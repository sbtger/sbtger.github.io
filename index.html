I'm Peng Zhang. Anred Lab is where I keep technical notes on operating systems, network and Android security, data-intensive applications, and the engineering problems I work on.

I'm Peng Zhang, the author of Anred Lab. I start with a concrete engineering problem and work my way down through the application, protocols, memory, and system calls, taking notes on what each layer explains.

The archive goes back to the site's Hexo days. It includes notes on operating systems, assembly, and embedded interfaces, along with hands-on work in Android security, network defense, and Python tooling. The data systems notes begin with reliability, scalability, and maintainability from Designing Data-Intensive Applications.
